# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary defines the domain language used across course participation and refunds.

## Language

### Course participation

**Enrollment**:
A learner's current access relationship with a course, distinct from the Purchase that granted it and the learner's Course Progress.

**Course Progress**:
A learner's history of advancement through a course, retained independently of current access.

**Team Seat**:
A course place purchased by a team for assignment to a learner.

**Watch Time**:
The amount of course video a learner has consumed, distinct from lessons opened or completed.

**Lapsed Learner**:
A learner who retains Course Progress for a course but no longer has an Enrollment in it.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request for Cadence to return the money paid for a Purchase, distinct from the Refund that may follow approval.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the request), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request that has not been approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Pending Refund Request

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase qualifies for a Refund Request under the applicable refund policy. Eligibility permits review but does not guarantee approval.

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Cadence has accepted, distinct from confirmation that the money has been returned.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Cadence has rejected.

**Resolution Note**:
Cadence's explanation for approving or declining a Refund Request, distinct from the learner's reason for requesting one.

**Refund**:
Money returned to a learner following an Approved Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Refund Request, Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
An approved Refund whose transfer has not been confirmed.

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund whose transfer to the learner has been confirmed.

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund whose transfer could not be completed; its Refund Request remains approved.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress
